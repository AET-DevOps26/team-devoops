# Problem Statement

Currently, club management is often distributed over many platforms for different purposes, which increases administrative work and is often impersonal. Administrative work is often complicated: manual billing, member tracking, and scheduling using distinct tools. Members on the other hand often receive generic feedback that does not enhance personal growth.
Our application provides a centralized and hierarchical club management system, that bridges the gap of efficient administrative work and individual member development. Key features include the management of club members, an automated financial module for billing, and give personalized feedback loop using AI. 
The intended users on one hand are the club organizers, that can use the app for organizational purposes as a all-in-one solution, reducing overhead by automating billing and maintaining a structured view of the organization.
On the other hand the members of the club that have a more organized overview of training sessions and events, as well as personalized feedback for improvements.
Additionally, the application allows trainers and mentors to document the progress of trainees easily and provide propper guidance.
This system uses GenAI as a personal assistant. It analyzes data for the user, unstructured trainer notes, attendance records and member profiles to tailor a progress report, that can be used to track successes and facilitate further improvement.

## Scenarios

If a club organizer wants to add 20 new members and colltect the annual fees, the organizer can easily register new members and billing is done automatically by the system.
Additionally can the users be assigned to a respective group in place.

If a member wants to track its training progress, it can simply view their AI feedback that allows them to determine recent progresses and potential advises for further training sessions. 

If a trainer wants to determine how well the trainees are doing, the trainer simply can get a summary by GenAI that allows him to get a proper overview and potentially provided further feedback for the individual trainees.
